2SendThe app sends the text with your sign-in.
3Who is it?Not signed in: stop, "Sign in to use Stack AI."
4Count itAdds 1 to today's count. Over 50: stop.
5Ask engineAdds the instructions and the secret key.

The key never leaves the server. Anyone can open an APK and read what is inside, so the paid AI key is kept on Supabase only.
The count is in the database. 50 a day per person by default. It resets at 00:00 UTC, which is 5:30 AM in India.
The engine is told how to answer. For a summary: exactly three short points, one takeaway line, in the article's language.

05 · Stops along the path · status

WHEN IT SAYS NO

Each check on the path can stop a request. The app then shows one short line in red where the answer would be. These are the exact messages in the code.

Step 3 · not signed in
Sign in to use Stack AI. Sign in
The link opens the Account screen.
Step 4 · limit reached
You've used today's 50 Stack AI requests. They reset at midnight (UTC).
That is 5:30 AM in India.
Step 2 · no internet
Couldn't reach Stack AI. Check your connection.
The rest of Stack works offline.
Step 5 · engine out of credit
Stack AI is paused: the AI account is out of credit.
Top up the engine's account.
Step 5 · engine busy
Stack AI is busy. Try again in a minute.
Too many requests at once.
Ask this PDF · file too big
This PDF is too big to send (the limit is about 22 MB).
Smaller PDFs work.

THREE STEPS TO SWITCH IT ON

1

Deploy the function

Put the ai function on Supabase and give it one engine key and a low daily limit.
You · about 20 minutes
2

Tell the app

Put the function's address in .env.local and rebuild the APK. The AI buttons appear.
You · one rebuild
3

Add Sarvam

Add the Sarvam engine to the function, plus a setting for each job so PDF chat stays on OpenAI or Claude.
Code · about half a day
