Why not the open Store in 10 days

Google's 14-day rule

A new personal Play account must run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days in a row before the app can be open to everyone. Nothing in this plan can shorten that. So the soft launch is the closed test: people join by a link, install from Play, and the 14 days count at the same time.
What can move the date

Google's first review

Google reviews the first closed-test build, and that can take a few days. Upload it on 4 October, before the AI work is finished, so the review runs while you build. If it is still not approved on day 8, start with Play's internal track, which takes up to 100 people and needs no wait.

The dates assume your Play Console account is already verified, which I could not check. If it is not, verification comes first and every date moves by the days it takes.

LEFT OUT OF THE FIRST TEN DAYS

Ships during the soft launch

Updates, not blockers

"Summarise in Hindi", "Explain simply", exam shelves, the app in Hindi. Each goes out as an update. Gates 8 to 11 go up as a second build on 6 October with a higher version code.
Free plan

Two Supabase limits to know

A free project pauses after about a week with no activity: press Restore in the dashboard. The free plan also has about 1 GB of file storage and Stack syncs PDFs of up to 50 MB, so check the storage figure every few days.

WHEN THINGS GO WRONG

ProblemWhat to do
Google's review is not done on day 8Start with Play's internal track: add up to 100 people by email and they can install at once. Move them to the closed track when it is approved. The 14 days count only on the closed track
Fewer than 12 people stay opted inThe 14 days do not complete. Invite more people the same day and keep the test running
Google sign-in fails on Play installsThe Android OAuth client for Play's app-signing SHA-1 is missing in Google Cloud (gate 7)
Nobody can sign in or syncThe free Supabase project may be paused. Open the dashboard and press Restore
The AI bill jumpsLower AI_DAILY_LIMIT in the Supabase secrets. It takes effect at once, with no new app
Hindi summaries are poorSend Indian-language summaries to the second engine for now, and report examples to Sarvam
Play rejects the buildRead the email, fix only that item, and reply through the Policy status page
