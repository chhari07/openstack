The idea in one line
Read in any language, think in your own. A student saves an English article or a coaching PDF, reads the points in Hindi, speaks a note in Hinglish, and revises it next morning. No big English-first reading app does this well, and that is Stack's opening.

02 · Language first

LANGUAGE FIRST

Language is the largest single change. Do it in this order, because each step makes the next one worth more.

1
Code · about a weekThe app itself in HindiMove every button and message into one text file per language, then add Hindi. The app follows the phone's language, with a choice in Settings. Keep words people already use in English: PDF, note, highlight, playlist.
2
Code · 1 to 2 daysAI answers in the reader's languageOne "AI language" setting. Summaries, "Explain simply" and Ask your Stack then answer in that language, whatever language the article is in. This is Sarvam's main strength.
3
Code · 2 to 3 daysNews in Indian languagesOffer ready feed packs at first launch: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi. "My feeds" already reads any RSS feed, so this is a list of feeds, not new machinery. Check that each publisher offers a public feed and allows it.
4
Code · 1 dayType that suits Indian scriptsArchivo has no Devanagari or Tamil letters, so the phone swaps in its own font. Choose one on purpose. Anek is an open-source family for ten Indian scripts and has the same wide-to-narrow range as Archivo. Indian scripts need more line height and no negative letter spacing.
5
Code · 2 to 3 daysHinglish is normalPeople type Hindi in English letters. Tidy must keep it as it is, and search should find "ज्ञान" when someone types "gyaan". Sarvam's speech model has modes for mixed and romanised speech.
6
Code · about a weekVoice in, voice outSpeak a note or a question; hear the summary in an Indian voice. Costs are in Stack_AI_and_Sarvam_AI.pdf, page 4.

WHICH LANGUAGES, IN WHAT ORDER

WaveLanguagesWhy
1Hindi and HinglishThe largest group, and the one you can check yourself
2Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, MarathiLarge reading and exam audiences. IAMAI-Kantar names Tamil and Telugu among the most used Indian languages online
3Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, PunjabiThe rest of the ten languages Sarvam's chat model supports
One rule
Never ship a language nobody has checked. A machine translation of the app's buttons reads as careless, and that does more harm than staying in English. For each language, find one reader among your testers who goes through every screen.

03 · How India studies

HOW INDIA STUDIES

Culture is habits as well as language. Indian students already have strong reading and revision habits. Stack should fit into them instead of asking for new ones.

Fits what is built

PDFs arrive on Telegram and WhatsApp

Coaching notes, question papers and newspaper PDFs move through Telegram channels and WhatsApp groups. Stack already imports from both. Put this at the front of the first-run screens: "Forward your study PDFs to Stack".
Small job

Exam shelves

Ready shelf sets for UPSC, SSC, banking, JEE, NEET, CUET, CA and state exams, with the right subjects as shelves. The reader picks an exam at first launch, as they pick interests today.
Small job

An exam date and a countdown

Enter the exam date once. Today shows the days left, and the daily review brings back more highlights as the date gets closer.
Medium job

Current affairs as a daily habit

Many aspirants read an editorial every morning and make notes. A "Today's editorial" card, highlight, then the morning review and the quiz from highlights turn that into one flow.
Small job

Revision words people use

In the Hindi version call the daily review रिवीज़न and the focus session अभ्यास (practice). Keep "Pomodoro" as a timer choice. Test the words with readers before fixing them.
Medium job

School books, the legal way

NCERT textbooks are free to download from NCERT's own site. Link to them from a "Textbooks" shelf so the reader downloads them into Stack. Do not put the files inside the app.
Later · Stack for Teams

The coaching batch

A teacher shares PDFs to a batch, students highlight and get quizzed, and the teacher sees progress. This is the Teams plan in the business plan, and coaching centres are its natural first buyers.
Small job

Study together

Share a highlight as a picture card to WhatsApp, with the source and the Stack mark. Students already send each other notes; this makes each share bring a new reader.
Who this points to
The business plan picked "students, developers and self-learners in India who save articles and PDFs and forget them". Exam aspirants are the part of that group with the strongest daily habit and the clearest reason to revise. Pick one exam for the soft launch and make Stack right for it, then widen.

04 · Look and feel

LOOK & FEEL

Identity should be felt, not shouted. Keep Stack's clean paper-and-ink design and add a few things that an Indian reader recognises as their own.

Brand

The line, in two languages

"Read it. Keep it. Build on it." next to पढ़ो। सहेजो। याद रखो। on the splash, the store listing and the website. One short line per language, checked by a native reader.
Brand

Patterns from the Stack blocks

The logo is four stacked blocks. Use the same blocks to draw kolam and rangoli style dot patterns for empty screens, streak cards and share cards. It stays Stack's own shape and reads as Indian.
Everyday

Indian time, dates and numbers

Count the day in IST so limits and streaks turn over at midnight in India. Show dates as 1 Oct 2026, and numbers in lakh and crore: ₹1,00,000, not ₹100,000.
Everyday

A calendar that knows India

A quiet card on festival days, and a "do not break my streak" pause for them. Add exam seasons: board exams early in the year, entrance exams after. Cover festivals of all communities, and let people switch the cards off.
Content

A starter shelf of Indian classics

A few public-domain works offered at first launch in old translations whose copyright has ended, for example the Thirukkural and the Arthashastra. Readers start with something on the shelf. Check each edition's copyright.
Content

Badges with Indian names

Stack has 12 badges. Name some after ideas people know, for example अभ्यास for a 7-day streak and एकाग्र for a full focus session. Avoid the names of gods and religious figures.
Sound

Focus sound that suits study

Offer simple study sounds made for Stack or released free to use, such as a tanpura drone or rain. Do not bundle film songs: they are copyrighted. The reader's own music stays as it is.
Story

Say where it is from

"Made in India by StackForge Labs. Open source. Works without an account." This is already true, and trust matters when 28 lakh cyber-fraud cases are reported in a year.
A caution on colours and symbols
Stack's green and orange already sit close to the national colours. Do not arrange them as the flag, and do not use the national flag or emblem in the icon or the logo: Indian law restricts their use by businesses. Patterns, language and names carry identity safely.

05 · Reach and price

REACH & PRICE

Being Indian in practice means working on the phones, networks and budgets that people have.

AreaWhat to doWhy
PhonesTest on a phone with 3 to 4 GB of memory. Keep the download small: the debug APK is about 15 MB todayMany students use low-cost Android phones with little free storage
NetworkAdd a "light mode" that skips images and video on mobile data. Offline reading is already builtData is cheap in India but coverage is patchy
PriceKeep ₹99 a month, ₹799 a year and ₹499 a year for students, as in the business planReadwise costs $9.99 a month, which is too much for most Indian students
PayingUse Google Play Billing, where people in India can pay with UPI. Offer the yearly plan firstMany students have no card
Store listingAdd a Hindi listing, then one per language as it shipsPlay shows a translated listing to phones set to that language
PrivacyKeep the consent screen before AI, the export, and delete-account. Translate them tooThe DPDP Act's full rules apply from 13 May 2027

WHERE TO FIND PEOPLE

Study groups

Telegram and WhatsApp

Exam groups already pass PDFs around. Offer a short guide: "Forward this PDF to Stack and revise it tomorrow". Follow each group's rules about promotion.
Campuses

Student ambassadors

One student per college or coaching centre, with free Stack Plus and a referral link. Start with your own college and two more.
Creators

Teachers who post in Hindi

Small study channels in Indian languages reach the right people. Give them the app and ask for an honest look, not a paid review.

TWO ENGINES FOR INDIAN LANGUAGES

Paid · fast to start

Sarvam AI

Chat in 10 Indian languages, speech and translation in 22. ₹100 free credit, then pay as you go. This is the engine planned for Stack AI.
Government · listed as free

Bhashini

India's public language platform offers translation, speech to text and text to speech for 22 languages, and is described as free for developers. It is worth testing as a backup for translation and reading aloud. I did not check its sign-up terms, limits or quality.

DO IT WELL

AvoidWhy it hurtsDo this instead
Unchecked translationsWrong or stiff Hindi looks carelessOne native reader checks every screen before a language ships
Religion and politics in the brandIndia is many communities; taking a side loses the restStay neutral. Celebrate every community's festivals or none
Stereotypes as decorationElephants, turbans and "namaste" everywhere read as a tourist's IndiaLanguage, habits and small patterns
The national flag or emblem in the logoTheir use by businesses is restricted by lawStack's own blocks and colours
Bundling books, songs or coaching PDFsCopyright complaints and Play removalsLink to the legal source; bundle only public-domain works
Showing whole news articles from publishersThe same copyright risk, in every languageHeadline, summary and a link; full reader only for what the reader saved
Ten languages at onceTen half-finished versions and nobody to check themHindi first, then one wave at a time
Trusting AI in every language equallyQuality differs by language, and a wrong summary costs trustTest 20 real articles per language and keep the report button
