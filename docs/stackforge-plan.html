01 · The verdict

THE SHORT ANSWER

Is a company building Stack as its first app a good idea for the market? Yes, with a narrower Stack. The market is moving your way. Indians are starting to pay for apps, AI and productivity lead that growth, and Pocket's shutdown left readers looking for a new home. The old working name, "openstack", clashed with a trademark and is now StackForge. Stack still tries to be four apps at once. Five decisions:

RenamedSTACK­FORGE
The company is now StackForge Labs. "OpenStack" is a registered trademark of the OpenInfra Foundation (Linux Foundation), so the old working name had to go. StackForge keeps Stack at the centre and says what the company does: it forges (builds) apps on one shared stack. Finish the trademark and domain checks before the Play Store listing and company registration (page 5).
NarrowSTACK
Keep the core that no giant owns: save anything → highlight → Stack brings it back so you remember it. Move music and the general news reader to the background. They compete with Spotify and Google News and create legal and policy problems (page 6).
BuildOPEN CORE
The company identity should be "an open, India-first stack of useful apps". The apps are free and open source at the core, and people pay for sync, AI and team features. This is how Obsidian, Readwise and many open-source companies earn money, and it builds trust for a new brand (page 8).
SequenceONE AT A TIME
Your report lists 14 problem areas. Build them in order, on one shared engine ("AI reads → code decides → a person confirms"). Stack first, BillBook second (a clear buyer who pays), then ScamShield (page 10).
MeasureUSERS, NOT FEATURES
You've already built six products. The next step is getting them to people, not writing more code. Set clear numbers and a date to decide: continue, change or stop (page 12).
One-line strategy
An open-source, India-first company that turns AI into small, trusted tools for everyday problems, starting with a reading app that makes you remember what you read.

03 · The name

THE NEW NAME

STACKFORGE
StackForge Labs · makers of Stack

The company was called openstack as a working name. OpenStack® is a registered trademark of the OpenStack Foundation (OpenInfra), now under the Linux Foundation, so the company is now StackForge Labs. Stack stays the main brand: the company forges apps, and each one is built on the same shared stack.

Why it works
  • Stack comes first, so the company and its main app share one brand
  • "Forge" = a place where things are built. It sounds like an engineering company and works in India and abroad
  • It fits every future app: Stack, BillBook by StackForge, ScamShield by StackForge
  • The block logo (stacked blocks) matches the idea of forging blocks
Watch out
  • "StackForge" was also the name of OpenStack's old project area (retired in 2015). It's not an active brand, but check it isn't still registered as a trademark
  • Short "stack" names are popular, so stackforge.com may be taken. Plan for stackforge.in, stackforgelabs.com or stackforge.app
  • Register the company as "StackForge Labs", not just "StackForge", so the name is more distinctive

04 · Stack · honest audit

STACK TODAY

Stack is well built: tested on emulators, with offline mode, sync, AI and native Android features. The risk isn't the code. It's focus, platform rules and getting it to users.

Strengths
  • Highlight → note → daily review: the one loop no big app does for free on Android
  • Local-first and works without an account: a privacy advantage
  • Share to Stack from any app, PDF reader, focus sessions, streaks
  • AI already wired in: Summarize, Ask this PDF, Ask your Stack, Tidy note
  • Web + Android + tablet from one codebase; backup and restore
Weaknesses
  • Four apps in one (news, music, PDFs, notes), so the store listing can't explain it in one line
  • Music and news are the least unique parts, and each fights a giant
  • No real users yet, so no data on what people actually use
  • AI defaults to Opus, which costs too much for a ₹99 plan
  • Solo founder with limited time
