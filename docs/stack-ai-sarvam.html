01 · Where things stand · checked 01.10.2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Stack AI is written but not switched on. Sarvam AI is not in the code yet. With both live, Stack can summarise, explain and listen in the reader's own language, and the everyday AI jobs cost about a third of what they cost on Claude Haiku.

PartStatusWhat I found in the project
Stack AI in the appBuiltFour features: Summarise an article, Ask this PDF, Ask your Stack, Tidy a note
Engines in the codeBuiltOpenAI and Claude, chosen with one server setting (AI_PROVIDER)
Daily limit per personBuilt50 a day by default, counted in the database. Resets at 00:00 UTC, which is 5:30 AM IST
The ai function on SupabaseNot deployedIts address answers "function not found" (404)
AI address in the appEmptyNEXT_PUBLIC_AI_URL is blank, so every AI button is hidden in today's APK
Sarvam engineNot builtNeeds about 40 new lines in one file: supabase/functions/ai/index.ts
Sign-in (AI needs it)PartlyEmail sign-in is on. Google is turned on in Supabase but not yet tested on a phone

THREE DECISIONS

Engines
USE
BOTH
Sarvam for the everyday text jobs, Claude or OpenAI only for PDF chat. Sarvam's chat model reads text, not PDF files, and Stack sends the whole PDF today. Three of the four features move to Sarvam without changing the app (page 3).
Cost
ABOUT
⅓
A summary costs about ₹0.09 on Sarvam, ₹0.31 on Claude Haiku and ₹1.23 on Claude Opus, which is the default in the code today. Changing the default model before launch matters more than anything else on the cost side (page 5).
Product
8 NEW
THINGS
Sarvam is more than a cheaper chatbot. It also turns speech into text, reads text aloud in Indian voices, translates, and reads scanned pages. That opens eight features that English-only reading apps do not have (page 4).
What this guide cannot tell you
Stack has no real users yet, so every rupee figure on earnings is a scenario, not a forecast. The cost figures are my estimates from typical text sizes and the listed prices; they were not measured on Stack.

03 · New things Stack can do

WHAT STACK GAINS

Eight features that become possible once Sarvam is in. None of them exists in the app today. Costs are per use, at the listed prices.

Chat · small job

1 · Summary in your language

A "Summarise in Hindi" choice on any article, also for the other nine languages. The reader picks the language once in Settings.
About ₹0.10 to ₹0.15 each. Hindi text uses more tokens than English.
Chat · small job

2 · Explain this line

Select a hard sentence and tap "Explain simply". Stack answers in plain Hindi, Hinglish or English, and the answer can be saved as a note.
About ₹0.03 each.
Speech to text · medium job

3 · Voice notes

Speak a note in Hindi, Hinglish or any of 22 languages. Saaras writes it down, then "Tidy" cleans it up.
A 30-second note costs about ₹0.25.
Speech to text · medium job

4 · Ask by voice

Ask your Stack or a PDF by speaking instead of typing. It uses the same microphone button as voice notes.
A 10-second question costs about ₹0.08, plus the answer.
Text to speech · medium job

5 · Listen in an Indian voice

Bulbul reads the summary aloud in a natural Indian voice. Keep the phone's free built-in voice for full articles: a 1,500-word article would cost about ₹27 on Bulbul.
A summary of 400 characters costs about ₹1.20.
Vision · large job

6 · Scanned PDFs become text

Photographed class notes and old scanned books turn into real text, so they can be highlighted, searched and read aloud.
₹0.50 per page, so ₹10 for 20 pages. Limit: 10 requests a minute.
Chat · large job

7 · PDF chat on Sarvam

The phone pulls the text out of the PDF and sends only the pages that match the question. Then PDF chat no longer needs Claude or OpenAI.
Does not work for scanned PDFs without feature 6.
Chat · medium job

8 · Quiz from your highlights

The morning review adds three questions made from your own highlights, in the language you read in. This was already planned in the business plan.
About ₹0.05 a day per person if the quiz is saved and reused.
What changes for the reader
Today Stack is an English reading app that happens to have Indian news. With these, a student can save an English article, read the points in Hindi, speak a note in Hinglish and hear it back. Build 1 and 2 first: they need no new screens, only a language choice and one new button.

FREE AND PLUS, WITH AI

Free

Enough to feel the value

  • 3 to 5 AI uses a day, on Sarvam
  • Summaries and "Explain this line", in English or Hindi
  • The phone's own voice for listening
  • No PDF chat, or one PDF question a day
Worst case ₹8 to ₹14 per free user per month.
Stack Plus · ₹99

Everything that costs money to run

  • 30 AI uses a day
  • PDF chat, Ask your Stack, quiz from highlights
  • Voice notes and the Indian voice for summaries
  • Sync across devices; scanned PDFs to text
Costs about ₹20 a month in AI for a typical user.
The rule that keeps this safe
One Plus user pays for about five free users at a limit of 5 a day, and for only one at a limit of 20. So free AI is a marketing cost: keep the free limit small until people start paying. Stack has no payment system yet. Subscriptions inside the Android app must use Google Play Billing, and that is a separate build.

06 · Build order

HOW TO SWITCH IT ON

Five steps, each one useful on its own. Stop after any step and Stack still works. "You" means a dashboard or a key only you can reach; "Code" is work in the repo.

A
You · 20 minutesPut Stack AI live as it isDeploy the function, give it one key and a low daily limit, put its address in .env.local, rebuild the app.
# in the project folder npx supabase login npx supabase link --project-ref <your project id> npx supabase functions deploy ai --no-verify-jwt npx supabase secrets set AI_PROVIDER=openai OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-... OPENAI_MODEL=gpt-5.4-mini AI_DAILY_LIMIT=10 # .env.local, then ./build-apk.sh NEXT_PUBLIC_AI_URL=https://<your project id>.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai
B
Code · half a dayAdd the Sarvam engineA new runSarvam next to runOpenAI in the function. Sarvam's chat API has the OpenAI shape, at api.sarvam.ai/v1/chat/completions. Add a setting per feature so PDF chat can stay on Claude or OpenAI while the other three use Sarvam. You add SARVAM_API_KEY to the function's secrets.
C
Code · 1 to 2 daysLanguage features"AI language" in Settings, "Summarise in Hindi", and "Explain simply" in the selection toolbar. No new Sarvam product is needed, only new prompts.
D
Code · about a weekVoiceA microphone button for voice notes and spoken questions (Saaras), and "Listen to the summary" in an Indian voice (Bulbul). Needs the microphone permission and a new line in the Play Data safety form.
E
Code · 1 to 2 weeksScanned PDFs and PDF chat on SarvamSarvam Vision for scanned pages, then text-based PDF chat. Do this last: it is the biggest job and PDF chat already works on Claude and OpenAI.

THINGS TO SETTLE BEFORE STEP B

QuestionWhat to do
Which model is the default?The code falls back to claude-opus-5-5 and gpt-5.5. Set a cheaper model in the secrets (AI_MODEL, OPENAI_MODEL) before any tester gets the app
What does the app call the engine?The "ask first" sheet shows NEXT_PUBLIC_AI_ENGINE. With two engines it must name both, for example "Sarvam AI and Claude"
Is Hindi good enough?Run 20 real Hindi and English articles through Sarvam and read every summary yourself before you turn it on for others

07 · Keep it safe

RISKS & RULES

RiskWhat could happenWhat to do
The bill growsA few heavy users or a bug use up the credit in daysLow daily limit, cheap default models, prepaid Sarvam credit, and a monthly spend limit on the Claude or OpenAI account
Credit runs outAI shows "Stack AI is paused: the AI account is out of credit"Check the Sarvam and Claude dashboards every Sunday. The rest of the app keeps working
Wrong answersA summary says something the article did notAlways show the page and note links, which Stack already does. Add a "Report this answer" button: Google Play asks for one in apps that make AI content
PrivacyText leaves the phone and goes to an AI companyStack already asks before sending anything and has one switch to turn AI off. Name Sarvam on the privacy page and in the Play Data safety form. Read Sarvam's data terms before launch
Sarvam is busy or downMore than 40 requests a minute, or an outageFall back to the second engine for that request. The function already has the pieces to swap engines
Voice costsReading whole articles aloud on Bulbul costs about ₹27 eachUse Bulbul only for summaries. Keep the phone's built-in voice for long reading
Reset timeThe limit resets at 5:30 AM IST, which confuses Indian usersCount the day in Indian time in the database function (ai_take) so it resets at midnight IST
Big PDFsPDFs over about 22 MB cannot be sentThe app already says so. Text-based PDF chat (step E) removes the limit for normal PDFs
